Service Territory Maps

Alabama law (Alabama Code § 37-14-1 to -17 and § 37-14-30 to -40) provides for assigned service areas for retail electric service. If you have questions about electrical service at a certain location, please contact customer service at 1-800-245-2244. Also, assigned service area maps are on file at the Alabama Department of Archives and History in Montgomery, Alabama.
