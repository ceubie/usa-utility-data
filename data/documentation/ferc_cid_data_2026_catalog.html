FERC Company Identifier Listing

The Commission's regulations require regulated or jurisdictional entities and some non-regulated or non-jurisdictional entities to obtain Company Identifier (CID) numbers in order to make required filings with the Commission including tariff filings and various required forms. Each regulatory program requires a unique CID number.
