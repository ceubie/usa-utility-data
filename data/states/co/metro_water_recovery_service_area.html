Our Service Area

Metro’s service area includes 65 municipalities and special districts across our 817-square-mile service area. Serving over 2 million people throughout City and County of Denver and parts of Adams, Arapahoe, Elbert, Jefferson, Morgan, and Weld counties, we are the wastewater treatment authority for most of metropolitan Denver.  

Our Interactive Geographical Information System (GIS) Map gives a very detailed and customized look at our service area and sewer systems including our interceptors and manhole locations. *Please note that this link will navigate away from our website.
