There has been an increase in phishing emails that appear to come from the Public Service Commission (examples include: louisianapublic.servicecommission@usa.com, LOUISIANA.PUBLICSERVICE@usa.com); however, legitimate Public Service Commission business will only come from an “la.gov” account. Please be aware of this potential scam and avoid providing any information to these fake domains.
