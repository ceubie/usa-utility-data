This page explains why and how energy costs change, shows Michigan’s national standing, and outlines how the MPSC improves affordability and reliability, reduces costs, and expands assistance. It also guides residents on understanding their utility bills and directs them to programs that support energy efficiency and affordability for Michigan families.
