Company Information

Hawaii Water Service (Hawaii Water) provides high-quality water to thousands of Hawaii residents and visitors. Our services include water distribution system construction and maintenance, meter-reading and billing, water quality testing and treatment, wastewater treatment, and customer education programs focused on water quality and conservation.

Hawaii Water Service is a subsidiary of California Water Service Group, which also includes California Water Service, New Mexico Water Service, Texas Water Service, and Washington Water Service. As a whole, the Group provides high-quality regulated and non-regulated utility services to approximately 2 million people in 100 communities. Company-wide, our employees share a commitment to being the leading provider of water and wastewater services and delivering quality, service, and value to our customers.

General Information

Hawaii Water is committed to supplying our valued customers with a reliable water supply and wastewater services that meets all water quality and environmental standards.

Ka’anapali

Since 2003, Hawaii Water has provided high quality water to Ka’anapali customers.

Kalaeloa Barbers Point

Since 2020, Hawaii Water has provided high quality water and wastewater utility services to the Kalaeloa Barbers Point area.

Kapalua

Hawaii Water acquired Kapalua Water Company and Kapalua Waste Treatment Company from Maui Land & Pineapple Company in 2021. Hawaii Water provides water and wastewater service to 1,000 customer connections in the Kapalua, Maui resort area. Kapalua’s water and wastewater utilities serve homes, hotels, condominiums, golf courses, restaurants, and other resort amenities in West Maui.

Keauhou

Since 2003, Hawaii Water has provided high-quality water and wastewater utility services to homes, condominiums, hotels, golf courses, and shops on Maui and the Big Island. We began serving as the operator of the Keauhou wastewater system in October 2018, and purchase of the system was closed in December 2022.

Kukio

Since 2008, Hawaii Water has provided high quality water and wastewater utility services to Kukio residents.

Poipu and Koloa

Hawaii Water began providing wastewater services to Poipu and Koloa customers in 2024.

Pukalani

Hawaii Water’s Pukalani Water Reclamation Facility is the first wastewater treatment plant in the State of Hawaii to use state-of-the-art flat plate membrane bio-reactor filtration (MBR) technology. It treats 200,000 gallons of wastewater per day to a high-quality effluent used to irrigate the neighboring golf course. Hawaii Water selected the flat plate membrane process because it has a very small footprint, is less costly to maintain, and produces water that can be reused without restriction, freeing up precious potable water supplies. The plant is more reliable and environmentally friendly than the old plant, which was originally constructed over 35 years ago.

Waikoloa

Since 2008, Hawaii Water has provided high quality water and wastewater utility services to homes, condominiums, hotels, golf courses, and shops at Waikoloa Beach Resort and in Waikoloa Village.
